# Swedish (Finland Swedish)

Free references for Swedish course versions, Finland-Swedish sources first.

## What it is

Institutet för de inhemska språken (sprakinstitutet.fi) publishes Finland-Swedish guidance, including Finlandssvensk ordbok (about 2,550 headwords, 4th edition 2008, free online) and klarspråk advice. Sweden's Isof publishes klarspråk guidance and writing rules (Snabba skrivregler free; Svenska skrivregler is a book). [IIS] [IIS-FSOB] [Isof-skrivregler]

## When it fits

Fits:
- Swedish versions for Finnish students, i.e. Finland Swedish: FSOB for finlandisms, Isof for punctuation and numbers where Finland has no rule.
- Klarspråk: instructions and course information. In explanations, only its clarity points; explanations read like a good non-fiction book.

Doesn't fit:
- A Swedish course aimed at readers in Sweden: Isof comes first there.
- Finnish and English text; lättläst, except as `course-material/references/writing/styles.md` **Easy-to-read** allows.

## Applies here

- **Finlandssvensk ordbok**: High. Which finlandisms to keep or avoid.
- **Klarspråk, Institutet för de inhemska språken**: Medium. Clarity of instructions and course information. [IIS-klarsprak]
- **Isof Snabba skrivregler, Svenska skrivregler**: Medium. Punctuation, numbers, abbreviations.
- **Isof klarspråk**: Low. Aimed at Swedish authorities. [Isof-klarsprak]

## How agents use it

- Finlandisms the teacher or course uses on purpose stay: report, don't change.
- Style precedence: `course-material/references/writing/styles.md` **Whose style comes first**.

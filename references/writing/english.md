# English

Free references for English course versions; the citable source for clarity and usage findings in English.

## What it is

The Guardian style guide, a free online A–Z of usage and style from a British newspaper. The European Commission's *How to write clearly* (2015) and its *English Style Guide* (8th edition 2016, updated May 2024; Irish/British usage). [Guardian-style] [EC-2015] [EC-DGT-2024]

## When it fits

Fits:
- English course text. The Guardian guide for usage and style, closest to a good non-fiction register; *How to write clearly* for clarity; the *English Style Guide* for punctuation, numbers and abbreviations when the course has no convention.
- Courses with many second-language readers: clear sentences help, terms stay.

Doesn't fit:
- Finnish and Swedish text.
- EU terminology (Style Guide Part II) unless the course is about the EU.
- House choices of the Guardian that serve journalism, not teaching (e.g. headline style). (editorial)

## Applies here

- **Guardian style guide**: High. Usage, word choice, capitals, hyphenation.
- ***How to write clearly***: High. Clarity findings.
- **English Style Guide, Part I**: Medium. Punctuation, spelling, numbers, abbreviations.

## How agents use it

- Spelling: infer the variety from the course and from how the teacher writes; never ask, never mix.
- The teacher and `course-rules.md` come first (`course-material/references/writing/styles.md` **Whose style comes first**). Never claim compliance.

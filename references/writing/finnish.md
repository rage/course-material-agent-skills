# Finnish

Free Finnish references: Kielitoimiston ohjepankki for form, Kotus's official-language guidance for clarity, and selkokieli, which is opt-in only.

## What it is

Kielitoimiston ohjepankki is Kotus's online language guide, updated continuously, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Its section "Hyvän virkakielen ohjeita" is written for authorities. Kotus (Kotimaisten kielten keskus) has been part of Opetushallitus since 2026. All free to read. [Kotus-ohjepankki] [Kotus-ohjeita] [Kotus]

## When it fits

Fits:
- Any Finnish text: the ohjepankki for compounds, hyphenation, quotation marks, numbers, abbreviations and names.
- The official-language guidance: course information, instructions, deadlines and grading, exercise questions, feedback and announcements. In explanations, only its clarity points (structure, sentences, terms); keep the voice, direct address and line of reasoning.

Doesn't fit:
- English and Swedish text.
- Quotations, code, glossary terms the teacher set, field jargon: report, don't correct.
- Legal wording; pages where terminology is the content.

## Applies here

- **Sana** (compounds, capitals, hyphenation, loanwords): High. Most common findings.
- **Merkit, numerot ja lyhenteet**: High. Punctuation, dashes, quotation marks, numbers, abbreviations.
- **Selkeät virkkeet, jäsennelty teksti** (virkakieli): High. Clarity findings.
- **Lause**, **Nimet**: Medium. Cases, number agreement, passive; names and their inflection.
- **Teksti**: Medium. Headings and lists.
- **Sanasto ja sananvalinta**, **Sopiva sävy, toimivat ohjeet ja kysymykset** (virkakieli): Medium. Consistent terms, keeping the subject's; instructions and exercise questions.

## Selkokieli

Read only when `course-material/references/writing/styles.md` **Easy-to-read** applies: a selkokielinen version the teacher asks for, or a course written for selkokieli readers.

Selkokieli is Finnish adapted for readers who find standard Finnish hard. Selkokeskus publishes writing guidance, the Selkokielen mittari (2.0, 2022, free, for expert evaluation) and the Selkotunnus mark. [Selkokeskus] [Selkokeskus-mittari]

- **Writing guidance**: High when asked. Writing the version, as a separate text the teacher reviews, since content is simplified.
- **Selkokielen mittari**: Medium when asked. Reviewing a finished version.
- **Selkotunnus**: never claim or imply it; never call a text selkokieli unless the teacher does.

## How agents use it

- Cite the ohjepankki page that supports a finding. Never call the course "hyvää virkakieltä".
- Style precedence: `course-material/references/writing/styles.md` **Whose style comes first**.

| [exercises.md](exercises.md) | You read or edit an exercise's name, task instructions or review instructions |
| [course-structure.md](course-structure.md) | You create, move, rename or remove pages, chapters or modules, or change chapter dates or page paths |

| [staging-and-publishing.md](staging-and-publishing.md) | You publish, hand over, discard or revert changes, or need to know what counts as risky |
| [rich-text.md](rich-text.md) | You write text into blocks: allowed inline markup, links, LaTeX, citations |

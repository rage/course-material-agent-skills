# What I can do

Offer three or four of these, picked for the course's state, in the teacher's language and in plain words. A draft course in progress: writing, research, walkthrough, claims. A running course: student feedback, links, exercises. A copied course: next run. A course with language versions: translation sync.

- **Write or extend material**: new pages and sections, with sources (`course-material`, `course-material-research`).
- **Research a topic**: find and weigh sources, propose references and glossary terms (`course-material-research`).
- **Review a page, chapter or the whole course** for many issues at once (`course-material-review`).
- **Read pages as a student would** and say where they'd get stuck; check that chapters teach their objectives (`course-material-student-walkthrough`).
- **Check facts** against current sources, and whether the course still teaches current practice (`course-material-claim-check`).
- **Check links**, images, files, videos and DOIs (`course-material-link-check`).
- **Check accessibility**: alt texts, headings, link texts, tables, captions (`course-material-accessibility-review`).
- **Proofread**: spelling, grammar, consistent terms, understandability (`course-material-language-review`).
- **Check exercises**: answer keys, unclear questions, feedback texts (`course-material-exercise-check`).
- **Restructure**: split, merge, move or reorder chapters and pages (`course-material-restructuring`).
- **Advise on course design**: structure, exercises, motivation, workload (`course-material-design-advice`).
- **Handle student error reports** and fix them in every language version (`course-material-student-feedback`).
- **Sync language versions** or check a translation (`course-material-translation-sync`).
- **Prepare a copied course for its next run**: dates, leftovers, readiness (`course-material-new-run`).
- **Edit settings, glossary and references**, and **publish or undo** changes (`course-material`).
